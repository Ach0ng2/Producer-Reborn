## 2. Audio Format Requirements (CRITICAL)

Many independent producers hit rejections here by assuming all aggregators accept raw WAV files.

### Required Format:
* **Format:** **FLAC** (Lossless) or **MP3 (320 kbps CBR)** stereo.
* **Bit Depth:** **16-bit**.
* **Sample Rate:** **44.1 kHz**.
* **Channels:** **Stereo** (not mono).

> [!WARNING] **RouteNote Does NOT Accept WAV for Standard Uploads!**  
> If you export your mix/master from your DAW as a `.wav` file, you **must** convert it to **FLAC (16-bit / 44.1 kHz)** or 320 kbps MP3 before uploading.  
> *Tip for DAWs (e.g., FL Studio, Ableton):* When exporting, select `.flac`, `16-bit int`, and verify your project sample rate is `44100 Hz`.

### Technical Audio Rules:
* **No Excessive Silence:** Avoid long dead air at the start or end of a track.
* **No Clipping / Harsh Distortion:** Masters should avoid harsh inter-sample / true-peak clipping.
* **Hi-Res Audio:** Supported only on special tiers (All Access/Label) for platforms like Tidal/Apple Music (24-bit, 88.2–192 kHz).

## 3. Cover Artwork Requirements

Non-compliant artwork is the #1 cause of *Action Needed* rejections on RouteNote.

* **Dimensions:** Exactly **3000 × 3000 pixels** (perfect 1:1 square).
* **File Format:** **.jpg** or **.jpeg**.
* **File Size:** Maximum **25 MB**.
* **Color Space:** **RGB** (never CMYK).
* **Image Quality:** Crisp, unpixelated, and undistorted (minimum 72 DPI, 300 DPI recommended).

### Strictly Prohibited on Artwork:
1. **No contact information:** No website URLs, email addresses, or social media handles (`@instagram`, `@tiktok`).
2. **No store/platform logos:** No Spotify, Apple Music, RouteNote, YouTube, or CD/Vinyl logos.
3. **No promotional text:** No "Out Now", "Free Download", "New Single", or "Exclusive".
4. **No prices or barcodes:** Your distributor generates barcodes automatically.
5. **Text Consistency:** If you include the Artist Name or Track Title on the cover art, **spelling, capitalization, and punctuation must match your release metadata 100%**. (Text-free artwork is always allowed.)
6. **Image Copyright:** Do not use celebrity photos without permission, copyrighted characters (anime/film/games), or unowned third-party photography.

## 4. Metadata & Title Style Guide

RouteNote enforces strict Apple Music & Spotify Style Guide formatting rules.

### A. Release & Track Title Formatting
* Use standard English **Title Case**.
* Do not capitalize short conjunctions/prepositions unless they begin or end the title: *a, an, and, as, but, for, from, in, of, on, or, the, to, with*.
* **NO ALL CAPS** (Example: `MY SONG` ❌ $\rightarrow$ `My Song` ✅), except official acronyms like `NASA` or `DJ`.
* **Do NOT include the primary artist name in the title:**
  * ❌ *Wrong:* `Artist Name - Midnight Drive`
  * ✅ *Right:* `Midnight Drive`
* **Do NOT include producer credits in the title:**
  * ❌ *Wrong:* `Midnight Drive (prod. by Producer)`
  * ✅ *Right:* `Midnight Drive` *(Add producers in the Contributor metadata field instead).*

### B. Collaborations (Featured, With, Remix)
* **Featuring:** Must use lowercase `feat.` with a period.
  * Format: `Track Title (feat. Featured Artist)`
* **With (Equal Collaboration):** Must use lowercase `with`.
  * Format: `Track Title (with Co-Artist)`
* **Remix:** `Remix` must have a capital 'R' and follow the remixer's name.
  * Format: `Track Title (Remixer Name Remix)`
* **Combinations:**
  * Order: Featuring $\rightarrow$ With $\rightarrow$ Remix.
  * First modifier uses parentheses `()`, subsequent modifiers use square brackets `[]`.
  * Example: `Track Title (feat. Artist A) [Artist B Remix]`

### C. Title Version Field
Version descriptors must go in the **Title Version** field, never inside the main title:
* Examples: `Radio Edit`, `Acoustic Version`, `Instrumental`, `Live`.
* Note: Never write `Original Version` or `Original Mix` (stores reject this on standard releases).

## 5. Samples, Loops, & YouTube Content ID Policy

Critical rules for producers using royalty-free loops (Looperman, Splice, Cymatics, etc.):

### A. Streaming DSP Distribution (Spotify, Apple Music, YouTube Music, etc.)
* **STATUS: ALLOWED & SAFE.**
* You may distribute tracks containing non-exclusive royalty-free loops to standard streaming stores as long as you have combined them with original production elements (beats, arrangement, vocals, etc.).

### B. Content Recognition (YouTube Content ID, TikTok, Meta Rights Manager)
* **STATUS: STRICTLY PROHIBITED.**
* **Why:** Content Recognition relies on *acoustic fingerprinting*. Because public loops can be downloaded by thousands of other producers worldwide, registering them for Content ID triggers *false copyright strikes* against other creators.
* **RouteNote Consequence:** RouteNote moderation will reject or flag your release if it contains non-exclusive loops and you check **YouTube Content ID** or **Facebook/Instagram/TikTok Rights Manager**.
* **Best Practice:** When selecting stores in RouteNote, **uncheck** YouTube Content ID for any track using public royalty-free loops. Keep standard streaming stores (Spotify, Apple Music, YouTube Music, etc.) selected.

## 6. ISRC & UPC Codes

* **UPC (Universal Product Code):** Barcode for the entire release (Single/EP/Album).
* **ISRC (International Standard Recording Code):** Unique identifier for each individual audio track.
* **Cost:** **100% Free.**
* **How to Use:** Leave the UPC and ISRC fields **blank** when creating a new release on RouteNote—official codes are generated automatically for free. Only enter existing codes if migrating a previously released catalog.

## 7. Release Timeline & Spotify Editorial Pitching

### Recommended Schedule:
1. **Internal Moderation:** RouteNote Free tier reviews typically take **7–21 business days**.
2. **Store Ingestion:** Once approved, stores like Spotify and Apple Music take **48 hours – 2 weeks** to ingest and schedule the release.
3. **Ideal Submission Window:** Submit your release **at least 4 weeks (28 days)** prior to your official Release Date (*Sales Start Date*).

### Spotify for Artists Pitching Rules:
* Your release must be approved and showing under **Upcoming** in Spotify for Artists.
* Editorial pitches **must be submitted at least 7 days before release day** (2–3 weeks prior is strongly recommended).
* Only unreleased tracks are eligible for editorial pitching.
* Submit via **Music > Upcoming > Pitch a song** at `artists.spotify.com`.

## 8. Claiming Your Spotify for Artists Profile

Two ways to claim your artist profile:

### Option 1: Via RouteNote Dashboard (Instant Connect)
1. Log in to RouteNote.
2. Go to **Promotion** or **Artist Services** $\rightarrow$ select **Spotify for Artists**.
3. Ensure you have at least 1 approved release under your artist name.
4. Click **Get Access** and authenticate with your personal Spotify account.

### Option 2: Via Spotify for Artists Portal
1. Visit [artists.spotify.com/claim](https://artists.spotify.com/claim).
2. Paste your Spotify Artist link (`https://open.spotify.com/artist/...`) or URI (`spotify:artist:...`).
3. Link your official social media accounts to verify identity.

## 9. Payouts, Royalties, & Taxes

* **Minimum Payout Threshold:** **$50.00 USD**.
  * Balances below $50 roll over automatically to subsequent months until the threshold is met.
* **Payout Schedule:** Processed automatically between the **15th – 20th of each month**.
* **Reporting Delay:** Digital stores report streams with an approximate **45-day delay** (e.g., January streams appear around March 15).
* **Payout Methods:**
  * **PayPal** (USD; ensure your account is verified to receive payments).
  * **Bank Transfer / Payoneer**.
* **Taxes:** RouteNote (UK-based) does not deduct withholding tax from international artists directly; artists remain responsible for reporting income in their own country of residence.

## 10. Release Statuses & Handling "Action Needed"

### RouteNote Status Stages:
1. **In Review:** Queued for RouteNote moderation review.
2. **Action Needed:** Moderation found an issue requiring your correction.
3. **Approved:** Cleared and delivered to Spotify, Apple Music, etc.
4. **Complete / Live:** Active and streaming across DSPs.

### How to Fix "Action Needed":
1. Open **Discography** $\rightarrow$ click the **Action Needed** tab.
2. Open your release and read the moderator notes at the top (or check your email).
3. Apply the fix (e.g., replace blurry artwork, fix title formatting, or re-upload 16-bit FLAC audio).
4. Click **Save and Resubmit** to return the release to **In Review**.
5. For disputes or technical questions, email `support@routenote.com` or `moderation@routenote.com` with your **UPC**.
