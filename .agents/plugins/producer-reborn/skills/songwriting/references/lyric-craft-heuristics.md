# Lyric Craft & Song Length Heuristics

Practical heuristics for pacing lyrics, estimating song length, designing hooks, and polishing singability.

---

## 1. Target Song Length vs. Lyric Line Count

Use these rough benchmarks to balance lyric density against track duration:

| Target Duration | Format / Use Case | Typical Structure | Approx. Line Count |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **30 – 60 Secs** | Short demo / Social teaser | 1 short Verse + 1 Chorus/Hook + Outro | ~8 – 16 lines |
| **1 – 2 Mins** | Compact / Lo-fi single | Verse $\rightarrow$ Pre-Chorus $\rightarrow$ Chorus $\rightarrow$ Outro | ~16 – 28 lines |
| **2 – 3 Mins** | **Pop / Streaming Standard** | V1 $\rightarrow$ Pre $\rightarrow$ C1 $\rightarrow$ V2 $\rightarrow$ Pre $\rightarrow$ C2 $\rightarrow$ Bridge $\rightarrow$ Final C $\rightarrow$ Outro | **~28 – 48 lines** |
| **3 – 4 Mins** | Expanded / Ballad | Extended Intro, Post-Chorus, Instrumental Solo, full Bridge | ~40 – 64 lines |
| **4+ Mins** | Narrative / Progressive | Extended storytelling or dynamic repetitive build | Concept-dependent |

> **Note:** Avoid cramming too many words into slow tempos (70–85 BPM). Conversely, upbeat tempos (120–130 BPM) can carry denser lyrics if the syllable rhythm stays tight.

---

## 2. Section Roles in Lyric Craft

* **Verse:** Must be specific. Ground the listener with concrete objects, places, actions, and sensory details. *(Instead of "I feel sad," try "Your keys are still on the counter, the coffee's gone cold.")*
* **Pre-Chorus:** Escalates rhythmic motion and emotional stakes, bridging the Verse story to the Chorus payoff.
* **Chorus:** Simple, repeatable, and contains the emotional thesis and song title.
* **Bridge:** Offers a twist—a shift in perspective, a confession, or a quiet realization before the final peak.
* **Post-Chorus / Chanted Hook:** Optional short refrain or vocal ad-lib motif that reinforces the main hook.

---

## 3. Memorable Hook Design

Strong hooks typically share 4 traits:
1. **Compact Title Phrase:** Punchy and concise (1–4 words).
2. **Distinctive Rhythm:** A rhythmic cadence easy to tap or sing back (e.g., syncopated accents or unexpected pauses).
3. **Image-Emotion Contrast:** Pairing a tangible everyday object with an abstract feeling.
4. **Strategic Placement:** Put the hook on the very first line or very last line of the Chorus.

---

## 4. 6-Point Lyric Revision Checklist

Before hitting record, run your lyrics through these 6 checks:
- [ ] **Does the Chorus summarize the song's core emotion in simple, memorable language?**
- [ ] **Does Verse 2 add new perspective or story progression**, rather than restating Verse 1?
- [ ] **Have stale clichés been replaced** with fresher, more personal imagery?
- [ ] **Do line lengths and syllable stresses flow evenly** when spoken at tempo?
- [ ] **Is every phrase comfortable to sing?** (Eliminate awkward consonant clusters that trip up the tongue.)
- [ ] **Is there enough breathing space** for the vocalist and room for instruments to respond?
